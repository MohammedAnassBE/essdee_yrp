Build Cloth Programs includes Cloth Excess Percentage (≥ 0), Item Yarn Recipe (unique positive ratios totalling 100%), Is Dyed Yarn and Same Finished Colour per finished colour, Cloth Kgs / 1 Kg Yarn (> 0), Knitting Process and conditional Dyeing Process. Dyed Yarn clears and disables Same Finished Colour. The request explicitly includes every required fabric route, retaining the profile's Knitting Output Dia. Default knitting colour and Dia-change process are owned by IPD Settings.

Consumers: boxes update PPO ordered rows through the Lot controller; colour-wise pieces feed cutting, work orders, BOM, cloth demand and WIP; both BOM tables feed Lot Purchase Summary; Fabric Details and cloth programs feed knitting / dyeing work orders. T&A or transfer locks both quantity grids. PPO plus existing colour-wise details makes boxes derived. Quantity mismatches are warnings, so legacy lots remain editable.
